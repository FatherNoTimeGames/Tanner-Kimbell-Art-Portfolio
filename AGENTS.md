# Project rules

- Use this repository as the portfolio source of truth. Preserve existing artwork, unrelated changes, and established decisions. Work in small stages; obtain approval before major restructuring, settings changes, or deployment.
- Publish only `docs/`. Use plain static HTML/CSS/JS with no paid services or unnecessary dependencies. Preserve accessible Android and desktop navigation.
- Only display artwork supplied by Tanner and explicitly approved for the relevant publication. Never generate portfolio images or references. Never automatically promote challenge artwork to the portfolio. Never overwrite originals without permission.
- This repository is public. Do not commit critique summaries, progress notes, unpublished paintings, or unrevealed planning anywhere in it under the current authorization. Excluding files from Pages does not make them private. No separate repository is authorized yet.
- The Drowned Stag (Week 1, creature design) remains Active until explicitly progressed. Source animals: red deer (Cervus elaphus) and a deep-sea anglerfish whose exact species must be selected before references. Preserve its brief and focus on anatomy, biological plausibility, and texture.
- Do not generate, preview, store, or reveal the next assignment until the current painting has been completed, critiqued, and acknowledged, or explicitly skipped. Only one assignment may be active. Alternate creature/environment categories; the next assignment category is environment, with subject and brief undecided.
- Intended assignment states: Active → Artwork Submitted → Critique In Progress → Awaiting Acknowledgment → Completed; also Skipped. Calendar time never advances assignments. Ask if completion or critique acknowledgment is uncertain.
- Unfinished work retains the current assignment, without backlog or penalties. Offer optional feedback or scope reduction. Final summaries are 100–200 words, only after completion and acknowledgment; never commit intermediate feedback or transcripts.
- Keep elapsed weeks, completion dates, completed/skipped totals, creature/environment totals, and critique status distinct. Do not invent a challenge start or completion date.
- The existing Saturday check-in automation is outside this implementation. Do not duplicate or modify it without permission. User-specified schedule begins November 21, 2026 around 9 AM America/Chicago; do not assume the existing automation has been audited against that schedule.
- Creature assignments combine two exact real species. Verify original photographic sources and species, aim for front/profile photographs of each, and record source URLs, attribution, and licensing. Omit unverifiable or missing views rather than substituting lookalikes. Only store photographs when licensing allows it. Never generate references or finished creature concepts unless explicitly requested (references must always be authentic).
- Keep new assignments achievable in 4–6 hours, preserving interpretation. Favor grounded dark fantasy, gritty realism, biological/cosmic horror, convincing materials, and atmospheric storytelling. Avoid repetitive subjects, cartoon exaggeration, and excessive eyes or tentacles. Do not automatically increase difficulty.
- Follow the authorized stages. Stage 2 establishes the portfolio and challenge pages; structured challenge tracking, verified references, and maintenance infrastructure follow separately. No hosting is currently approved.
